19/11/2005 & Always

💗 Happy Birthday, bacha! 🎂✨

Hey bacha ❤️,

Aaj ka din mere liye bhi special hai, kyunki aaj us insaan ka birthday hai jo meri life mein ek alag si smile aur ek alag si feeling lekar aayi hai. 🫶🏻💖

Sabse pehle, Happy Birthday to you, bacha! 🎂🎀 May your life always be filled with happiness, success, beautiful memories and all the little things that make your heart smile. Tumhari har wish poori ho aur tumhari smile hamesha aise hi bani rahe. ✨

Pata hai, kuch log life mein achanak aate hain, lekin dheere-dheere itne special ban jaate hain ki unse baat karna, unki smile dekhna aur unke baare mein sochna bhi din ka favourite part ban jaata hai. Mere liye tum kuch aisi hi ho. ❤️

“Tumhe paana hi meri mohabbat ki shart nahi,
Tumhara khush rehna bhi mere liye kisi dua se kam nahi.” 🌷

Aur aaj tumhare birthday par main tumse ek baat kehna chahta hoon, jo shayad mere dil mein kaafi waqt se hai. 🥹💌

bacha, I really like you. Tum mere liye sirf ek naam nahi, balki ek aisi feeling ho jise main words mein poori tarah explain nahi kar sakta. Main nahi jaanta ki future kya hoga, lekin itna zaroor jaanta hoon ki tumhe aur achhe se jaanne, tumhare saath beautiful memories banane aur tumhari life ke special moments ka hissa banne ki meri dil se wish hai. ❤️

So, bacha… will you give me a chance to be more than just a friend? Will you let me be the person who makes you smile, supports your dreams and stands beside you through life's little ups and downs? 🫶🏻💗

Main tumse perfect hone ka promise nahi karunga, lekin honest rehne, tumhari respect karne aur tumhari feelings ko samajhne ki poori koshish zaroor karunga.

Aur agar tumhari feelings mere jaisi nahi hain, toh bhi main tumhare answer ki respect karunga. Kyunki tumhari khushi aur tumhari choice mere liye important hai. 🌸

Aaj ke din bas itna chahta hoon ki tum khoob haso, apne saare dreams poore karo aur life tumhe woh saari khushiyan de jinki tum haqdar ho.

One last thing, bacha… ❤️

“Hazaaron chehron mein ek sukoon sa lagta hai,
Tumhara naam bhi dil ko ek alag ehsaas deta hai.
Birthday tumhara hai, par dua meri hai,
Tumhari zindagi khushiyon se bhari rahe, bas khwahish meri hai.” 🎀

Happy Birthday once again, bacha! 🎂💗
Keep smiling, keep shining and keep being the beautiful person you are. ✨

And maybe, just maybe… this birthday becomes the beginning of a beautiful new chapter for us. 🥹❤️

With lots of care, feelings and a little hope,
Someone who genuinely wishes to see you happy. 💌🌷
